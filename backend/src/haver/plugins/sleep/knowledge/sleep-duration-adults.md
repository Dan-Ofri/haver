# How much sleep adults need
Source: https://jcsm.aasm.org/doi/10.5664/jcsm.4758

Adults aged 18 to 60 should sleep 7 or more hours per night on a regular basis to promote optimal health.
Regularly sleeping less than 7 hours is associated with poorer health outcomes.

Summarized in our own words from the 2015 consensus recommendation of the American Academy of
Sleep Medicine and the Sleep Research Society (Watson et al., Journal of Clinical Sleep
Medicine).
