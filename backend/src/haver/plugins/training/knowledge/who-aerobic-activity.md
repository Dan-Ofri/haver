# Weekly aerobic activity for adults
Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK566045/

Adults aged 18 to 64 should do at least 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic
physical activity per week, or at least 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic
activity, or an equivalent combination of both.

Doing more than 300 minutes of moderate activity per week can bring additional health benefits.
