# Protein intake for building muscle
Source: https://bjsm.bmj.com/content/52/6/376

For people doing resistance training, a total protein intake of about 1.6 grams per kilogram of
body weight per day captured most of the extra muscle gain from protein. Intakes above that
gave no clear further benefit, although the upper bound of the estimate was about 2.2 grams per
kilogram per day.

Summarized in our own words from a 2018 systematic review and meta-analysis (Morton et al.,
British Journal of Sports Medicine).
