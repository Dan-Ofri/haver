# How often adults should do strength training
Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK566045/

Adults should do muscle-strengthening activities at moderate or greater intensity that work
all major muscle groups on two or more days a week, because these bring additional health
benefits.

Summarized in our own words from the WHO 2020 guidelines on physical activity and sedentary
