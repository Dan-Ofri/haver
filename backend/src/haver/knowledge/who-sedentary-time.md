# Limiting sedentary time
Source: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK566045/

Adults should limit sedentary time, meaning waking time spent sitting, reclining or lying down
with very little energy use. Replacing sedentary time with
physical activity of any intensity, including light intensity, brings health benefits.
